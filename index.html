Campus Sustainability Tracker

A smart platform where students contribute to making the campus more eco-friendly by reporting sustainability actions.

🌍 Our Mission

Encourage sustainable behaviour across campus by tracking recycling, energy saving, tree planting, and eco-friendly transportation.

📊 What This Platform Does

• Track sustainability reports from students • Show environmental impact through CO₂ savings • Provide analytics dashboards • Reward eco-friendly behaviour through points and leaderboard
